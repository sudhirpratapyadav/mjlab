# Slide-Window — asset provenance (CL-V2, W2-b)

## What it is

A **560 x 440 mm anodised-aluminium horizontal-slider vent window**: a 40 mm outer
frame, one fixed light and one 240 x 360 mm sliding sash on the front track, 4 mm glass
in both, a centre track upstand, and a 140 mm vertical bar pull on the sash's leading
stile. Built procedurally (PartNet-Mobility unreachable — STATUS D8).

## What was kept, and what changed

**Kept bit-identical from cl25** (the classical teacher scores 1.000 and its face-push
standoff is derived from these):

* `handle` collider — box `pos="-0.04 -0.10 0" size="0.012 0.012 0.07"`;
* `base_site` / `object_site` at `(-0.04, -0.10, 0)`;
* `window_base` mocap, `handle` body, `window_slide` along +y;
* the frame's 0.22 m half-height, so
  `MECHANISM_DROP_BELOW_MOUNT["window"] = 0.220` and the mount z = 0.250 are
  **unchanged** — the pull rides at exactly the same height as in cl25.

**Changed:**

1. The cl25 "window" was a 20 x 640 x 440 mm back plate with a 20 x 300 x 400 mm green
   translucent slab sliding across it. It is replaced by a real two-light slider: a
   four-section outer frame (`window_body` = the far jamb, the geom
   `ee_window_collision` names; plus `window_jamb_near`, `window_head`, `window_sill`),
   a fixed glazed light, a centre track, and the sliding sash.
2. `window_pane` is now the sliding **sash** collider (box, 24 x 240 x 360 mm) rather
   than a bare slab; the sash's glass and stiles are separate visual meshes.
3. Joint range 0 .. 0.25 -> **0 .. 0.24 m**: 0.24 m is the real travel of a 240 mm sash
   in a 480 mm opening. The command target (0.22 m) and threshold (0.03 m) are
   unchanged and sit comfortably inside the range, so success still needs >= 0.19 m.
4. Sash mass 2.48 kg -> **1.05 kg** (a real aluminium sash, not a solid 1000 kg/m³ box).
5. `<compiler angle="radian" meshdir="assets" texturedir="assets"/>` added.

## Teacher

`classical/slide_window.py` is unchanged in strategy and every constant re-derives to
its cl25 value, because the handle collider, the frame's half-height and therefore the
mount height are all identical. `PUSH_STANDOFF` is exact: bar half-width 0.012 plus the
closed pad block's 0.0152 half-thickness = 0.0272, rounded to 0.028.
